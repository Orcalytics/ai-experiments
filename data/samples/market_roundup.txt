Most U.S. stocks ticked higher on Wednesday, a day after breaking their seven-day winning streak, while the price of gold pushed further past $4,000 per ounce.

The S&P 500 rose 0.1% and hovered near its all-time high set on Monday. The Dow Jones Industrial Average was down 20 points, or less than 0.1%, while the Nasdaq composite was up 0.3%.

AST SpaceMobile jumped 17% after Verizon Communications agreed to use its space-based network to support its cellular customers beginning in 2026. Verizon slipped by 0.1%.

Markets remain near record levels, but trading has been muted following the recent U.S. government shutdown, which delayed major economic reports such as the monthly jobs report. Without these data points, stocks have drifted as investors await more signals regarding continued interest rate cuts from the Federal Reserve.

Another major driver pushing markets to record highs is the growing excitement around artificial intelligence technology.

Poet Technologies rose 4.7%, extending its 23.5% surge from Tuesday after announcing it raised $75 million to fuel expansion. The company builds high-speed optical engines and other hardware used in AI systems.

Dell Technologies added nearly 6% after highlighting new AI-related growth opportunities. AI stocks more broadly have rallied sharply this year: Nvidia is up nearly 40%, Oracle has risen 70%, and Palantir Technologies has surged 140.9%.

These rapid gains have drawn comparisons to the 2000 dot-com bubble, though supporters argue today's AI stocks are backed by real profit growth. Even so, the Bank of England warned of the risk of a sudden correction in tech stocks inflated by AI enthusiasm.

Gold continued its strong year, climbing further above $4,000 per ounce. Investors have turned to gold as protection against inflation, and the metal is up more than 50% this year. Concerns about rising government debt, global political instability, and expectations of more Fed rate cuts have fueled demand.

The Federal Reserve will release minutes from its latest meeting later today. At that meeting, the Fed cut its main interest rate for the first time this year and signaled more cuts may be coming as it attempts to support a cooling job market while keeping inflation in check.

European stock indexes rose after a weak session in Asia. In the bond market, the 10-year Treasury yield dipped to 4.10% from 4.14%.
